## The day in one image

**Hero — Missiles land in Poland.** Two Russian missiles and a suspected drone came down in south-east Poland during a massive Russian bombardment of Ukraine. NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte visited Warsaw and vowed the alliance will "defend every inch of Polish territory". Overnight, Russian strikes on Kryvyi Rih killed a 7-year-old boy and a paramedic.

**COP31 in overtime** — climate talks in Antalya, Turkey, entered a second day of overtime; a new draft deal aims to avert collapse.

**Gulf on edge** — Bahrain said Iran set fire to an industrial site; two oil tankers were hit near the Strait of Hormuz.

**Cartel boss captured** — Mexico seized Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, "El Chapo Isidro", on the FBI's most-wanted list.

**Markets slump** — the S&P 500 suffered its worst week since April 2025; traders price in a Fed hike in December.

**EU–Indonesia deal** — a trade deal signed after a decade of talks, ending tariffs on 98% of EU exports.
